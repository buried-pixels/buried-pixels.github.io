Every gamer has a list.

Not of their favorites — of the ones that *should* have been favorites. The ones that got buried under a AAA marketing blitz, released on the wrong platform at the wrong time, or simply never got the review coverage they deserved.

That's what Buried Pixels is about.

We're here for the B-tier heroes. The cult classics that never found their cult. The indie darlings that flew too close to the sun and got forgotten on the way down.

Expect honest reviews, deeply personal top lists, and the occasional essay on why the games industry keeps overlooking brilliance in favor of the familiar.

If you've ever finished a game and thought *"how does no one know about this?"* — you're in the right place.

Let's dig.
